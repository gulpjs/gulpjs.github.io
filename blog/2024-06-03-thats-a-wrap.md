The Gulp Dev Survey Comes to a Close.

<!-- truncate -->

The Gulp Developer Survey is officially closed. We want to thank each and every one of you who completed the survey! In total, we received over 600 survey responses and are excited to review what everyone has to say.

Without the editors who graciously shared the survey link with their readers, we know we would not have had such a good turn out. So shout out to the following newsletters for including the Gulp Developer Survey:

✨ [Web Weekly](https://webweekly.email/) — “Your friendly web dev newsletter.”

![Banner for the Web Weekly newsletter](/img/web-weekly-banner.png)

✨ [JavaScript Weekly](https://javascriptweekly.com/) — “A newsletter of JavaScript articles, news and cool projects.”

![Banner for the JavaScript Weekly newsletter](/img/javascript-weekly-banner.png)

✨ [Codrops Collective](https://tympanus.net/codrops/collective/) — “Weekly news about qualitative new projects and libraries in web development and design.”

![Banner for the Codrops Collective newsletter](/img/codrops-collective-banner.jpg)

✨ [The Repository](https://www.therepository.email/) — “What’s happening in WordPress — and what everyone’s saying about it.”

![Banner for The Repository newsletter](/img/the-repository-banner.png)

✨ [JAMstacked](https://cfe.dev/newsletters/jamstacked/) — “An email update on the evolving web development ecosystem.”

![Banner for the JAMStacked newsletter](/img/jamstacked-banner.png)

✨ [Console](https://console.dev/latest) — “A free weekly email digest of the best tools for developers.”

![Banner for the Console newsletter](/img/console-banner.png)

✨ [The WP Weekly](https://thewpweekly.com/) — “The Weekly Newsletter for WordPress Professionals.”

![Banner for The WP Weekly newsletter](/img/wp-weekly-banner.jpg)

If you’re missing out on developer happenings, we highly recommend checking out these wonderful sources!

Over the next few weeks, I will be cleaning and analyzing the data for the Gulp team. We will be sharing the results here as soon as they are finalized, so keep your eyes peeled for the next blog post if you consider yourself a #dataisbeautiful nerd like myself. 🤓
