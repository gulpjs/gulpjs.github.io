Welcome to our freshly relaunched blog! You may have noticed that we are now directly hosting our blog on the [gulpjs.com](/) domain.

In a world of corporations obsessed with the [enshittification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshittification) of their platforms, the gulp team is methodically moving away from platforms we don’t control. One of the easiest places to change was self-hosting the blog on our own infrastructure instead of relying on Medium.

We’ve migrated our old posts from Medium and plan to release a series of posts about our current development efforts leading up to v6. So please subscribe (rss, atom) with your favorite reader software to stay updated!
